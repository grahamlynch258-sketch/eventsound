# Photo prep guide — what goes where

Make these folders on your desktop and sort photos into them before uploading.
Each folder name matches a tab in **Admin → Image Library**, so uploading is
just: open the matching tab → drag the folder's photos in → write a one-line
description per photo (it asks you before uploading).

Photos are compressed automatically on upload — don't spend time resizing.
Anything from a phone or camera is fine; just prefer **landscape** shots.

Notes:
- **You never tag a photo as "the hero" — position does it.** Within each tab
  the cards are numbered, and #1 is the banner (or the first slide). A new
  upload lands at the bottom; click its ⬆ arrow until it reaches #1. The badge
  on the card will read "#1 · banner" (or "· first slide") so you can see at a
  glance which photo the page is actually using.
- **Two things are managed elsewhere:**
  - The numbered content slots on the LED Walls, LED Screen Hire, and Musical &
    Theatre pages ("Service type 1", "Use case 3"…) live under **Admin →
    Manage Images** — slots are labelled individually there.
  - The main **Gallery page grid** lives under **Admin → Gallery** with its own
    categories (Conferences, Awards & Galas…).
- The city pages (Dublin/Cork/Galway/Belfast/Limerick/Athlone) reuse the LED
  Walls and Conference AV tab images — no separate folders needed.
- Banner photos get a dark overlay with white text on top — photos with a
  clear, not-too-busy middle area work best.
- After changing the **first** Homepage Slideshow image, press **Publish site**
  on the admin Dashboard so the fast-loading version of the homepage updates.

## Writing the one-line descriptions (alt text)

This text is read by Google and by screen readers — it's part of why the site
ranks. Say what's actually in the photo, naturally, with the service and place
when true:

- ✅ "LED video wall behind the main stage at a corporate awards night in Dublin"
- ✅ "Pipe and drape backdrop with uplighting at a gala dinner"
- ❌ "IMG_4032" · ❌ "photo1" · ❌ "led wall hire dublin best price" (keyword stuffing)

One sentence is plenty. If you can't think of one, describe the photo like
you'd describe it to someone on the phone.
