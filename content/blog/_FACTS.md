## House rules digest (full version: docs/blog-style-guide.md)

- Irish/British English. Confident, calm, candid about trade-offs. No
  "cutting-edge/seamless/unforgettable" without proof. Explain jargon once.
- 14–20 words average sentence; paragraphs 2–4 sentences.
- One H1 (the title). H2s organised around decisions. FAQ section (3–5 Qs)
  only where genuinely useful. End with an intent-matched CTA, not a generic
  "contact us today" — see the style guide's CTA menu.
- Answer the core question in the first ~120 words.
- Every post: link its money page in the first third; link exactly two
  sibling posts where naturally relevant; one link to /contact/ in the CTA.
- Lengths: technical buyer guides 1,400–2,200 words; practical guides and
  checklists 1,000–1,500; the case study 700–1,200.
- Prices/specs: state "as of August 2026" where quoted.
- Do NOT claim ISO certifications, awards, memberships or sustainability
  programmes — we have none to claim. TUV applies to staging only.
